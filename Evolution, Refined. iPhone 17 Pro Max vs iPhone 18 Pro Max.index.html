Which one makes more sense?

Verified and reported advantages for each, without assuming newer means better.

iPhone 17 Pro Max

  • Lower launch price: $1,199 vs $1,299 (256 GB).
  • Lighter, at 233 g (Apple) vs a reported 249 g.
  • Charging and front camera details are fully published.
  • Cosmic Orange and Deep Blue finishes.

iPhone 18 Pro Max

  • A20 Pro chip, named by Apple.
  • Reported up to 45 hours of video playback vs 39 hours.
  • Reported variable aperture on the main camera.
  • Black, Glacier and Burgundy finishes, plus Apple C2 modem.

Choose by what you will use: the 17 Pro Max if price and weight matter most, the 18 Pro Max if battery and the new chip do. Items marked Not confirmed could change the picture.
